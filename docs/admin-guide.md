# FTCCI website admin: staff guide

This guide is for FTCCI staff who update the website. You don't need any technical knowledge. It describes the admin as of November 2026; the screens will get a new look, but the steps stay the same.

## Signing in

1. Open the admin address your Super Admin gave you (for example `https://www.ftcci.in/admin`).
2. Choose how to sign in:
   - **Password:** enter your email address and password, then press **Login**.
   - **Email code:** enter your email address and press **Email me a code**. A 6-digit code arrives in your inbox within a minute (check spam if not). Type it in and press **Login**. Each code works once, for 10 minutes.
3. Forgot your password? Press **Forgot Password?**, enter your email, and follow the link in the email within 30 minutes.

Good to know:

- After 5 wrong passwords, password sign-in is paused for 15 minutes. You can still sign in with an emailed code.
- You're signed out after 8 hours without using the admin. Anything already saved is kept.
- Never share a code or password. FTCCI staff will never ask you for one.
- To set or change your password, or to sign out on other computers, open your name at the top right, then **My account**.

## The dashboard

The first page shows how many events, news stories and publications are on the website, and how many drafts are waiting. **Quick Actions** take you straight to the most common tasks; **Recent Activity** lists the latest edits.

## Adding or editing an event, news story, publication, Review issue or vacancy

1. In the left menu, choose the section (for example **Events**).
2. Press **+ Add New Event**, or press the title of an existing one (or its pencil icon).
3. Fill in the form. Fields marked * are needed before publishing. The **Live Preview** on the right shows how the card will look on the website.
4. Press **Save Draft**. Nothing appears on the website yet.
5. Press **Preview** to see the real page in a new tab (the preview link works for one hour).
6. Press **Publish** when it's ready. It appears on the website within a minute.

While you edit an existing item, the admin saves your changes by itself every few seconds ("All changes saved"). Saving never changes the website: only **Publish** (or **Publish changes**) does.

### Publishing later

In the **Publishing** panel, choose a date and time under **Publish later** and press **Schedule**. The item publishes itself at that time. **Cancel schedule** stops it.

### Taking something down

In the **Publishing** panel:

- **Unpublish** takes it off the website and keeps it here as a draft.
- **Archive** removes it from the website's lists, but old links to it still work. Past events are a good use for this.
- **Delete** moves it to the bin. A Super Admin can restore it.

Every button that removes something asks you to confirm first.

### Going back to an earlier version

**Version history** lists every publish and recent save. Press **Restore** next to a version to bring it back as the draft; your current draft is kept in the history too. Then publish it.

## Website pages

**Website pages** holds the fixed pages: the home hero (video or image carousel), home page, About, Membership, Services (including the hall tariff used by the booking form), Knowledge Centre, Media, Privilege Card, Careers and the footer. Each works like the editor above: change it, preview it, publish it.

## Images, PDFs and videos

- In a form, use **Upload** to add a new file, or **Choose from library** to reuse one.
- The **Media Library** lists every file. The tabs show which page uses it. Press a file to change its title or **alt text**: a short description of the photo for people who can't see it.
- Limits:
  - Images: JPG, PNG or WebP up to 20 MB.
  - PDFs: up to 100 MB.
  - Videos: MP4, MOV or WebM up to 200 MB. They are compressed for the website in the background, which takes a few minutes.
- A file that is used on a page can't be deleted. Replace it there first.

## Website submissions

**Hall Bookings**, **Membership**, **Event Interest** and **Contact Messages** list what visitors sent through the website. The yellow number shows how many are new.

1. Open one with **View**.
2. Contact the person.
3. Set the status to **Contacted** or **Closed**, and add notes for your colleagues.

If an email to the visitor couldn't be sent, the page says so; press **Try again**.

## For Super Admins

- **Staff accounts:**
  - Press **+ Add staff member**, enter their name and email, and choose **Admin** or **Super Admin**. They get an email explaining how to sign in.
  - **Disable** stops someone signing in straight away. Use it when a person leaves; **Enable** undoes it.
  - **Allow password** lifts a 15-minute password pause.
  - You can't change your own role or disable yourself; another Super Admin has to.
- **Activity log:** shows every sign-in and change, who made it, and when. Use the filters to find something.
- **Bin:** holds deleted items and files. **Restore** puts one back exactly as it was. A published item goes straight back on the website.
